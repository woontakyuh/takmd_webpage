# Jamsu lower crossing

The runtime replaces the two baked Jamsu meshes with a continuous low deck, raised navigation profile, two-lane asphalt, red cycle surface and open railings. The existing Banpo upper deck, paired columns, lights and traffic remain. The lower bearing caps align to the retained upper support stations. Four static material batches add no animation or new texture downloads.

References checked 12 September 2026:

- [Seoul Hangang overview](https://english.seoul.go.kr/service/amusement/hangang/overview/): stacked Banpo/Jamsu relationship and Jamsu approximately 2.7 m above normal river level.
- [Seoul Future Heritage](https://futureheritage.seoul.go.kr/futureHeritageMeet/futureHeritage/view.do?htNo=308): upper bridge 1,490 m × 25 m; lower floodable crossing.
- [Korea Road Association, Road Traffic 182](https://www.kroad.or.kr/journal/182th_07road_essay1.pdf): original 30 m lifting sections and later raised navigation passage.
- [Seoul Institute side photograph](https://data.si.re.kr/photo/06o04605ba20000) and [under-deck photograph](https://data.si.re.kr/photo/06o04604ba40000): built deck edges, repeated supports and separated road/cycle surfaces.
- [Existing dimensions reported with Seoul's competition](https://www.newspim.com/news/view/20240510000446): 795 m × 18 m. Proposed pedestrian-gallery imagery is not used.

The scene retains its existing approximate Banpo axis, so both levels remain centered together. OSM ways 1085504592 and 1091571021 were checked, but their precise footprints are not substituted independently: that would separate the lower bridge from the retained upper geometry. This is a visual reconstruction, not a surveyed structural model. Central rise (6.8 m), crest extent, bank approaches, section thickness and railing dimensions are photograph-informed estimates. The low deck top uses y=2.7 with scene water at y=0. Mild warm night edge emission approximates nearby installed lighting; it is not a new physical floodlight or proposed intervention.
