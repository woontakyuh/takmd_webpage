# Listening disc artwork

All six disc faces use the album covers embedded in the owner-supplied MP3 originals. The owner explicitly selected matching album-cover labels rather than photographed commercial disc printing. These are custom circular labels, not claimed physical-CD print reproductions.

1. Asoto Union — Sound Renovates A Structure / Think About’ Chu.
2. Brown Eyes — Reason 4 Breathing? / 비오는 압구정.
3. GIRIBOY — 땡큐 / 하루종일 (Band Ver.). The previously approved JPEG and its circular composition are unchanged.
4. Two Ton Shoe — Resoled / Paper Bag.
5. Radiohead — The Bends / High and Dry.
6. John Splithoff — Make It Happen (Deluxe Edition) / Raye.

Embedded JPEGs were extracted without redrawing. Labels other than the approved CD 3 use WebP delivery copies at a maximum 768px, preserving aspect ratio. The circle crops centrally without stretching; the geometry supplies its own spindle hole. Original MP3s and extracted JPEGs are unchanged in private sources/evidence.

Covers texture actual annular 3D meshes. The chassis, moving clamper, spindle, controls and display are rendered and interactive. Audio is requested only after playback intent. Per-track hashes and durations are recorded in the private release evidence audio-assets.json.
