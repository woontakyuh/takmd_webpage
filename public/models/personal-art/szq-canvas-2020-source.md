# SZQ Gallery canvas, 10 December 2020

Owner-supplied painting, photographed in IMG_1453.HEIC; IMG_1454–IMG_1464 were inspected for surface relief, canvas edges, and the handwritten date. The owner made this work while painting alongside his wife at SZQ Gallery, Seoul. The painting and its signature are original source pixels, not generated artwork.

Estimated display dimensions: 420 × 560 × 20 mm, updated following the owner's correction that this canvas is larger than the adjacent calendar artwork. These are display estimates, not measured dimensions. The exposed white canvas edge is part of the work: no added picture frame or glass.

The upright original is registered from its four outer canvas corners into a 1536 × 2048 sRGB WebP, preserving the complete heart, paint strokes, border, and date. No generative fill, sharpening, or recoloring is used. A 768 × 1024 high-pass grayscale map supplies a small bump/displacement lighting response; it is an artistic approximation, not a measured 3D scan. Its amplitude is limited to 1 mm, with pigment gradients and the signature border excluded from relief. Photographed shading is retained in the color source.

Regeneration: decode IMG_1453.HEIC to an upright full-resolution PNG with macOS sips, then run `node scripts/import-szq-canvas.mjs /path/to/upright.png` from the project root. The script records normalized registration corners. Originals and room backgrounds are not published.

The inspection photograph `szq-gallery-couple-2020.webp` is a WebP encoding of the owner's supplied 622 × 735 PNG showing both spouses holding their paintings at SZQ Gallery on 10 December 2020. Its full composition, people, artwork and original date/location text are preserved without cropping or generation. It loads only inside the opened artwork caption and uses the existing photo viewer for enlargement.
