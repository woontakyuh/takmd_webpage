The Lagavulin file uses Contentful's delivery transform at 1000 pixels in WebP format. Bottle outlines follow the reference silhouettes. Runtime UV coordinates map the product surface onto matching three-dimensional bottle geometry; packaging and photo backgrounds are outside the sampled bottle outline. These are visual reconstructions rather than manufacturer CAD models. Booker's batch sticker and numeric ABV line are suppressed with adjacent unmarked surface samples because a specific batch is not established.
