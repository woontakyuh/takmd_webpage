The classic Bowmore and Lagavulin files are resized to 1500 pixels in width. Bottle outlines follow the reference silhouettes, with flat cap crowns and round/faceted cross-sections. Glass and whisky use physical scene-lighting materials without product photography or baked reflections. Only actual paper-label regions are mapped onto the curved body. Hibiki uses 24 glass facets and a separate clear stopper. The archived Redbreast reference is the official 700 ml front, resized to 1500 pixels; neither Redbreast version is rendered in the current cabinet. These are visual reconstructions rather than manufacturer CAD models. Booker's batch sticker is omitted and its numeric ABV line is suppressed with adjacent blank paper because a specific batch is not established.

The active display contains six favorite whiskies plus Castarède Bas Armagnac XO in a dedicated cabinet: Lagavulin 16, Booker's, Ballantine's 30, Hibiki Japanese Harmony Master's Select, Bowmore 17 White Sands, GlenDronach 18, and Castarède XO 20 ans d'âge. It represents the owner's taste, not a synchronized owned-inventory claim. Other archived references in this directory are not loaded by the collection.

The official current packshot has an English age statement and unopened neck sleeve; the owner supplied the French-label opened version with an exposed cork and short black ridged cap. The new SVG contains only cream paper and reconstructed typography, retaining the photographed French wording. It contains no photograph pixels, hand, room, glass, or reflections. The 370 mm height and approximately 84 mm body diameter are visual reconstruction estimates. A closed round lathed glass body, volume absorption, cork and 32 physical cap ridges respond to scene lighting. The bottle's roughly 55% fill follows the owner's reference rather than the full official packshot.

The user’s cabinet photograph was a reference for the earlier owned-bottle pass; its pixels are never used as textures. The current cabinet is an original design adapted to the room, with room for additional favorites. Cream labels reduce sampled photographic paper shading, while the brown Bowmore 17 and blue GlenDronach 18 print colors are preserved. Only paper-label patches carry maps; glass, whisky, closures and trim use modeled geometry and scene lighting.
