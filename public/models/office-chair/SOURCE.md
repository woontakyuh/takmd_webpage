# Office chair asset source

- Model: **Office Chair** by **Nisu**
- Canonical source: https://www.3dmodelscc0.com/model/office-chair
- Download mirror and license record: https://opengameart.org/content/office-chair-1
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Source download: `office_chair.zip` from the OpenGameArt record above
- Source contents: FBX mesh plus 2048 px base-color, height, metallic, normal, and roughness maps

The repository copy is a web derivative. The FBX was converted to GLB with
FBX2glTF 0.9.7. The base-color map was resized and recolored through the authored
metallic mask: upholstery is warm tan while the frame and mechanisms remain
neutral graphite. The normal, metallic, and roughness maps were resized to
1024 px and retained as PBR inputs. Geometry was not
procedurally reconstructed. The source is a generic office chair and is not
identified as a Herman Miller product.

Checked 2026-09-06.
