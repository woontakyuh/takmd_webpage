# CSRS 2022 book surfaces

The front cover and three interior surfaces are flat raster exports of the official CSRS 50th Annual Meeting Abstract Book, matched against the owner's photographs IMG_0818, IMG_0824 and IMG_0825 on 2026-09-09.

Official source: https://www.csrs.org/UserFiles/am22-abs-bk-v5-HIRES.pdf
Archive: https://www.csrs.org/meetings/meeting-archives

- csrs-cover.webp: PDF page 1, original front cover.
- csrs-program.webp: PDF page 13, printed page 11, Thursday November 17, 2022, 12:56–1:01pm, Paper 23. A translucent multiply highlight preserves the owner's photographed mark over Woon Tak Yuh, MD. Text and page layout are unchanged.
- csrs-paper-left.webp: PDF page 88, printed page 86, Paper 23 abstract.
- csrs-paper-right.webp: PDF page 89, printed page 87, Paper 23 tables and figure.

These four images are 1334×2000 WebP exports. The source page aspect ratio is 2:3. The complete PDF is not part of this site's public assets. The rear cover and shelf spine remain derived from the owner's original photographs. No generated or reconstructed text is used.
