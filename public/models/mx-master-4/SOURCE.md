# MX Master 4 reference model

Reference-modeled for TakMD from the five user-supplied pale-gray Logitech MX Master 4 photographs, saved in `docs/reference-assets/mx-master-4/`. This is an original visualization mesh, not an official Logitech CAD asset or a relabeled model of an earlier mouse.

Official physical dimensions: length 128.15 mm, width 88.35 mm, height 50.8 mm. [Logitech specifications](https://support.logi.com/hc/en-ch/articles/28321406604439-Specification-MX-Master-4), checked 2026-09-07.

Geometry: asymmetric lofted palm housing from longitudinal reference sections; separate broad left thumb shelf, inset haptic surface, top and horizontal knurled metal wheels, button divisions, USB-C opening, logi mark and battery LED. Front is local -Z, thumb shelf is -X, contact plane Y=0. There is no mouse cable.

The source photos remain local reference material and are not used as flattened image planes in the scene. The sculpted model is an approximation of the supplied product, with no claim of manufacturer endorsement. No third-party mesh was reused: the available exact-name community mesh was visibly distorted and the good earlier-generation model prohibited derivatives.

Thumb wheel correction (2026-09-07): the horizontal wheel is seated in a Boolean-cut aperture through the main shoulder and thumb shelf. Its axis follows the photographed 18-degree side slope; approximately 1.9 mm of the outer arc projects past the gray lip. The earlier external mounting block and square center band were replaced with an integrated gray shoulder and round machined band. The forward side button was lowered 2.5 mm to preserve clearance. The top MagSpeed wheel, overall size, and pale-gray material colors are unchanged.
