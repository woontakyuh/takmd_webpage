# Office product references, 2026-09-08

These are original scene reconstructions, not official manufacturer CAD downloads.

- DCW éditions Mantis BS1 B: https://dcw-editions.com/products/mantis-bs1 and https://presscloud.com/file/91/912263132981229/Technical_Sheet_MANTIS_BS1_B.pdf . Technical sheet visually inspected: round 290 mm base, 270 mm shade, articulated long reach. Chosen over the existing AJ because the base can sit beside the recliner and the long arm points over its reading seat without crossing the TV.
- Philips Hue Signe gradient floor: https://www.philips-hue.com/en-us/p/hue-white-and-color-ambiance-signe-gradient-floor-lamp/046677803513 . Height/footprint 1458 × 111 × 111 mm from official Hue catalogue: https://www.assets.signify.com/is/content/Signify/Assets/philips-lighting/romania/20221010-philips-hue-catalogue-2022-eng-1.pdf . One corner light plus existing/under-USM strips avoids crowding.
- Lutron Palladiom: https://www.lutron.com/us/en/window-treatments/palladiom ; outside mounting supported by https://assets.lutron.com/a/documents/palladiom_shading_system_spec_submittal.pdf and https://www.lutron.com/TechnicalDocumentLibrary/048736.pdf . Custom outside mounting with side/bottom overlap avoids the previous center gap and tight aperture fit.
- String System hallway: https://www.stringfurniture.com/en/series/system/combinations/hallway/bundle_h_s_12_12 . White 200 × 30 cm wire floor panels, 58 × 30 cm shelves and hanging rods inform a two-bay room configuration. Clothing models preserved.
- River material/software provenance: see river-quality.md. Composition remains an approximation; no claim that a complete licensed Han River model was imported.
