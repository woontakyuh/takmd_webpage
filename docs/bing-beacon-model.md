# Owner's Bing Beacon

The reference model is [Bing Beacon](https://bingsurf.com/collections/beacon). The manufacturer's 9ft6 size chart gives 23.25in width and 3in thickness, implemented as 2.8956 × .59055 × .0762m. Intermediate rocker and rail sections are reconstructed from the owner's photographs, not factory CAD.

IMG_0947.HEIC and IMG_0981.heic define the amber bottom, original Bing/60 graphics and crossing tail-lamination boundaries. IMG_0983.HEIC defines the darker perimeter. Both opposing cloth laps contribute one darker layer; their overlap produces the darkest fan. The deck keeps its photographed wax pattern and a separate laminate cap beneath it. Graphic source pixels are preserved rather than retyping the logos.

The foil silhouette follows the [Bing × Flying Diamonds 8.5in Flex](https://bingsurf.com/collections/fins/products/bing-x-flying-diamonds-fin-8-5-flex) reference, scaled to the owner's requested 9.5in fin height. The side texture is the supplied 60th-anniversary photograph with its actual 60, 9.5 and Larry Allison markings. The foil has a 9.2mm maximum root thickness, a rounded leading edge, a fine trailing edge and a full midsection tapering toward the tip. Thickness is a visual reconstruction from the owner’s feedback, not a measured manufacturer specification. This is the requested reference combination, not a claim that the current Flex is sold with this anniversary print.

The anniversary reference places its retaining screw on the tailward tab, opposite the newer Flex product photograph. The small visible screw sits approximately 8mm behind the foil root, through a modeled tab. The receiver's long faces are subdivided and seated against the same bottom curvature as the fin so its center cannot disappear beneath the board. Hidden transverse-pin hardware is not separately rendered.

`scripts/prepare-bing-surfboard.mjs` reproduces the binary from the private Down originals and supplied anniversary photograph. Source paths remain outside the public artifact. The GLB contains seven mesh parts and six embedded WebP maps. The updated binary is 3.34MB, approximately 0.37MB above the preceding version; there is no dynamic cloth simulation or per-frame texture generation. Evidence and SHA256 receipt are in `.omo/evidence/calendar-audio-refinement-2026-09-11/surfboard/`.

The in-room story uses the original IMG_0282 purchase photo: 30 September 2019, Bing factory, Encinitas. Purchase date/location and the woman’s role as the factory owner and shaper’s wife are owner-supplied context.
