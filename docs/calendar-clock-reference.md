# Calendar clock reference

The clock borrows the TWEMCO QD-35's rounded black housing, thin metallic inner rim and five-card hierarchy: weekday/date/month above hours/minutes. Reference photograph and product specification: [DETAIL QD-35 Black](https://www.detail.co.jp/view/item/000000000684).

The real product is approximately 305 × 216 × 78 mm. This is a design adaptation, enlarged to 780 × 460 × 78 mm for readability in the office overview, not a one-to-one product replica. The time cards are 225 × 175 mm, up from 146 × 123 mm, with heavier numerals. Small seconds and helper labels were removed. The original local calendar/time and reduced-motion-aware flip transitions remain.

Only the numeral mask emits softly at night; the black case and card surfaces respond to room light. Opaque face/card layers retain depth separation to prevent the previously observed distant stripes. Center height 2.82 m gives a 2.59 m bottom and 3.05 m top, clearing the TV top at 2.565 m and the crown moulding bottom at 3.078 m. Rounded extruded faces preserve the large corner radii independently of the shallow case depth.

Verification artifacts are in `.omo/evidence/calendar-clock-qd35-2026-09-08/`.
