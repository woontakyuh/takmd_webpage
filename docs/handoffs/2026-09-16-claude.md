## This checkpoint

- **Safari blank readers (root cause of the "TV opens the old page" report).** `TvScreenReader` and `MonitorScreenReader` used drei's `Html transform` mode. Its inner layer is scaled to metres (about 0.006) and magnified back through CSS perspective; WebKit rasterises the layer at the tiny scale, so Safari showed a blank TV and monitor while the reader's controls stayed tappable. Taps on the blank TV landed on invisible controls, most often the `Teaching & training` link below the screen, whose Education panel is the legacy `/education` content. Both readers now use a screen-aligned overlay (`center` + viewport-aware `distanceFactor`); they only mount once the camera faces the screen head-on, so geometry matches the bezel to 0.1 px in Chromium and paints in WebKit. Chromium-only QA had never exercised this path.
- **Header = office title.** The upper-left brand block (mark, name, caption) is gone. The italic office title with the `TAKMD / A PLACE TO THINK` kicker is the page `h1` upper-left; guided views rename it and keep the close control beside it. Guided-view buttons stay lower-right; Living CV / Contact stay upper-right.

## The education address (2026-09-17)

Removing the reader's "Teaching & training" link stopped new visits to the retired education panel, but the addresses it had already pushed stayed in visitors' history. `?exhibit=education&detail=/education%23overview` still rendered that panel, so any back navigation during a television session — Safari's edge-swipe back while scrolling the archive, most often — brought the old page back. That is why the fault survived the link removal and why tapping alone never reproduced it.

`officeViewFromUrl` now drops a stored `/education…` detail and `officePathView` sends `/education` to the television with or without its hash; other stored details are untouched. `src/components/studio/officeNavigation.test.mjs` covers the address rules and the phone gate loads the stored address and asserts the television opens. When a route is retired, retire its stored addresses in the same change.
