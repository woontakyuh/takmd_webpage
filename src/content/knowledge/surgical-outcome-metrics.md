# Surgical Outcome Metrics

Patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) are essential tools for evaluating surgical effectiveness and tracking recovery.

## Common Metrics

- **VAS (Visual Analog Scale)** — pain intensity, 0–10 scale
- **ODI (Oswestry Disability Index)** — functional disability assessment
- **EQ-5D** — health-related quality of life
- **JOA Score** — Japanese Orthopaedic Association score for myelopathy

## Why PROMs Matter

Systematic PROM collection enables evidence-based practice improvement and contributes to large-scale outcome registries.
